🀄 MahMahMia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

MahMahMia is a score tracker for Mahjong and Rummy, available at mahmahmia.com and as a mobile app. There are no accounts and no sign-up. It is built to collect as little as possible.

What stays on your device

Your game data — player names, scores, and round history — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere.

What we collect

When a game ends, the app sends one anonymous statistics record so we can understand how MahMahMia is used: a random app-generated ID (not linked to you or your device identity), game type, player count, number of transactions, game duration, device type, language, screen width, app version, time zone, hour of day, score spread, and which features were used. Player names and scores are not included.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties
  • No personal information collected — no names, emails, contacts, or location

Where statistics are stored

Anonymous statistics are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on secure servers.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all local game data and the random ID. Because statistics records contain no personal information, they cannot be traced back to you; if you would still like records associated with your random ID removed, contact us.
